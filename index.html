CampusSage · SCSE4041

Find the rule. See the source.

CampusSage is a student project prototype for answering university policy questions from supplied policy text. It keeps the question, source version, matching clause, and model prompt together.

Open the policy workbench View the source notes

1. Add a source

Paste public policy text and its title or academic year. Keep section labels intact.

2. Ask a question

Use a fictional example. The workbench finds a likely clause or flags a weak match.

3. Review the answer

Copy the grounded prompt to a language model, then check its quote and section.

Prototype limits: The bundled example is from the 2024-25 Examination Manual and may be out of date. The workbench does not connect to a model or verify current rules. Do not enter personal student information or use it to decide an individual student's eligibility.
