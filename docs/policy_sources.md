# CampusSage policy source for the prototype

## Source used in the current test fixture

- Bennett University, *Examination Manual 2024-25*, Section 10, “Attendance Requirements.” Official PDF: https://www.bennett.edu.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Examination-Manual-2024-25-.pdf
- The university registrar's notices page lists academic booklets, policy documents, and notices: https://www.bennett.edu.in/notices-registrar-office/

The prototype's sample text is a short paraphrase of Section 10(i)-(v)(c), with the manual's section labels preserved. For the final evaluation, use the current policy version confirmed by the team or faculty. The 2024-25 manual does not establish what rule applies in a later academic year.

## Scope and privacy

Use public policy text and fictional questions only. Do not upload student IDs, grades, medical documents, contact details, or personal cases. The prototype must not determine an individual student's eligibility; it can quote a rule and direct case-specific decisions to the relevant university authority.
